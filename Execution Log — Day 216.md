**Topic:** Observability



**Concepts Learned:**

* Observability
* Logs
* Metrics
* Traces
* Production Monitoring



**Key Insights:**

* Observability transforms debugging from guesswork into evidence-based analysis.
* Logs, metrics, and traces answer different but complementary questions.
* Effective production systems are designed to be observable, not just functional.
* Observability concepts extend naturally from cloud systems to embedded and IoT applications.



**Systems Engineering Principle:**

Design systems that expose enough signals for engineers to detect, diagnose, and resolve problems efficiently.



**Progression:**

Health Checks → Production Monitoring → Observability
