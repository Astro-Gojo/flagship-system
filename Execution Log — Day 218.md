**Session Type:** Core Session



**Topic:** Incident Response



**Concepts Learned:**

* Incident
* Incident Response Lifecycle
* Detection
* Assessment
* Mitigation
* Resolution



**Key Insights:**

* Incidents are prioritized based on user and business impact.
* Restoring service is usually more urgent than finding the exact root cause.
* Mitigation and resolution serve different purposes.
* Honest degradation (clearly indicating unavailable data) is safer than presenting incorrect information.



**Systems Engineering Principle:**

Design operational processes to restore availability quickly while preserving the information needed for permanent fixes.



**Progression:**

Observability → Alerting → Incident Response
