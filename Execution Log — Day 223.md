**Session Type:** Micro Session



**Topic:** Backup vs Replication



**Concepts Learned:**

* Backups
* Replication
* Data Recovery
* Failover



**Key Insights:**

* Replication supports availability.
* Backups protect historical data and recover from corruption or accidental deletion.



**Systems Engineering Principle:**

Availability and recoverability solve different problems.



**Progression:**

High Availability → Data Protection
