**Day 276 — Execution Log | Date : 22/09/2026**



**Session:** Micro



**Topic:** Priority scheduling, fairness, starvation, aging



**Core Concepts:**

* Priority gives important workloads preferential access to resources.
* Continuous high-priority arrivals can starve lower-priority workloads.
* Aging gradually improves the priority of long-waiting tasks.
* Fair scheduling and time slicing help distribute CPU access.



**What I Learned:**

* Scheduling isn't simply about maximizing priority.
* Systems must balance responsiveness for critical workloads with fairness.



**Key Systems Insight:**

Protecting critical work too aggressively can create starvation elsewhere.



**Foundation Principle:**

Priority must be balanced with fairness.
