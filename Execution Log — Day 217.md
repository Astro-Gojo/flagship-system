**Concepts Learned:**

* Alerting
* Alert Rules
* Thresholds
* Alert Fatigue
* Actionable Alerts



**Key Insights:**

* Alerts notify engineers about sustained, meaningful problems.
* Time-based thresholds reduce false alarms.
* Excessive alerts lead to alert fatigue and reduce system reliability.
* Effective alerts rely on observability data for context.



**Systems Engineering Principle:**

Design alerting systems that prioritize actionable information over noisy notifications.



**Progression:**

Observability → Monitoring → Alerting
