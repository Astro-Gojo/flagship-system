**Topic:** Virtual Machines vs Containers



**Concepts Learned:**

* Virtual Machines
* Containers
* Guest OS
* Host Kernel
* Resource Overhead



**Key Insight:**

VMs generally provide heavier machine-level virtualization, while containers provide lighter application-level isolation.



**Systems Engineering Principle:**

Choose the isolation mechanism according to the system's requirements and resource constraints.
