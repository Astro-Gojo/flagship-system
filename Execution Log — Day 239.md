**Session Type:** Micro Session



**Topic:** Horizontal vs Vertical Scaling



**Concepts:**

Vertical Scaling, Horizontal Scaling, Distributed Complexity



**Key Insight:** 

Scaling up simplifies architecture but has hardware limits; scaling out provides greater capacity at the cost of distributed-system complexity.



**Systems Engineering Principle:**

Choose scaling strategy according to capacity requirements and architectural constraints.
